# Course Introduction, Objectives and Outcomes

COURSE DESCRIPTION:

This course provides an introduction to Quantum Mechanics, Nanotechnology, Solid State Physics, Lasers and Fiber Optics, emphasizing their fundamental principles and applications. It begins with basics of quantum and nanotechnology, exploring quantum confinement effects, nanomaterial synthesis methods, and recent advancements like carbon nanomaterials, nano-sensors, and energy-harvesting technologies. In quantum mechanics, students learn about wave-particle duality and practical applications like quantum computing and edge computing.  Then it covers the basic crystal structure of solids, and then extended to superconductors. The lasers and fiber optics unit covers the principles of laser operation, types of lasers, and the functionality of optical fibers, including their applications in sensors and communication. Real-world advancements like green nanotechnology, biosensors, and nanocomposites are highlighted. Self-study topics such as carbon nanotubes, high temperature superconductors and fiber-based sensors are included for advanced learners. This course equips students with knowledge to understand and contribute to emerging technologies in science and engineering.

COURSE OBJECTIVES:

1.       The course is designed to enhance students' knowledge about fundamentals of physics and enable them to apply it in practical situations.

2.       It will provide the conceptual understanding of various domains of advanced engineering physics and their applications in respective fields of engineering.

3.       It will give thorough knowledge of modern physics to enable students to disseminate knowledge in academic areas.
