# Course Syllabus and Suggestive Readings

Unit-1

Quantum Physics and Nanotechnology

Contact Hours:15

Quantum Physics &Nanotechnology

Planck’s hypothesis and radiation law, Photoelectric Effect, Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle, Wave-Particle Duality, de-Broglie Matter Waves, Wave Packet, Wave Function

Introduction to Nanotechnology, Quantum Confinement Effect, Classification of Nanomaterials, Methods of Synthesis-Top down and Bottom-up Approach: Ball Milling & Sol-Gel Method.

Recent Inventions

●        Quantum Computing- Weather Forecasting, Cryptography

●        Edge Computing- IoT, Driverless Cars, and Healthcare

●        Carbon Nanomaterials-Carbon-based Nano Additives.

●        Semiconductor Nanodevices-Nanomanipulators, and Nano-transistors.

●        Green Nanotechnology- Bioremediation and Biotransformation of soil, Wastewater, Oil Spills, and Other Toxic Pollutants.

●        Nanocomposites for Coatings, Additives, Catalysts, and Structural Components.

●        Nano-sensors: Enabling Contamination Detection, Environmental Measurements, Remote Sensing, and Communication.

●        Nanoencapsulation for Drug Delivery, Bioimaging, Theragnosis, Immunization.

●        Nanomaterials for Water Treatment.

●        Nanotechnology for Energy Harvesting-Electrodes, and Solar Cells.

Self-Study Topic

Black body radiations, Tunnelling effect, Carbon Nanotubes, Graphene, Fullerenes

Unit-2

Solid State Physics

Contact Hours:15

Solid State Physics

Classification of Solids, Crystal Structure, Bravais Lattices, SC, BCC, FCC Structures, Atomic packing efficiency, Coordination Number and other parameters. Miller Indices, Interplanar spacing, Bragg’s Law.

Superconductors, Critical Temperature, Meissner Effect, Type-I and Type-II Superconductors. Applications of Superconductivity.

Recent Inventions

●        High-Performance Solid-State Batteries with Superionic Electrolytes.

●        Graphene Superconductivity: Bio Sensors, Foldable Smart Phones

●        Improved Performance of Electronic Devices with Minimum Energy Losses

Self-Study Topic

High-Temperature Superconductors, Highly efficient PV cells.

Unit-3

Lasers and Fiber Optics

Contact Hours:15

Lasers

Introduction, Spontaneous and Stimulated Emission of Radiation, Einstein coefficients, Conditions for Amplification, Main Components of Laser, Ruby Laser, Semiconductor Laser, Holography

Fiber Optics

Principle of Optical Fiber, Basic Structure, Types of Fibers, Acceptance Angle and Cone, Numerical Aperture, Losses in Fibers.

Self-Study Topic

Sensors based on Fibres and Lasers
