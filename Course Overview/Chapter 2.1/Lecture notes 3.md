Coordination number

The coordination number of an atom refers to the number of nearest neighboring atoms that are in direct contact with it in a crystal structure. Essentially, it indicates how many atoms surround a particular atom within the lattice. The coordination number depends on the crystal structure, with different structures having different coordination numbers.

For simple cubic: In a simple cubic crystal, each atom has 6 nearest neighbors. That’s half as many nearest-neighbors as close-packed structures like FCC and HCP. Each nearest neighbor is an atomic bond, so SC’s 6 nearest neighbors imply a weak, unstable structure.

Effective number of atoms

For Simple cubic:

A simple cubic unit cell consists of 8 corner atoms. Each and every corner atom is shared by eight adjacent unit cells. Therefore, one corner atom contributes 1/8 th of its parts to one unit cell. Since, there are eight corner atoms in a unit cell, the total number of atoms is 1/8 x 8=1. Therefore, the number of atoms in a simple cubic unit cell is one.

For BCC

A corner atom in a body centered cubic unit cell is shared by eight adjacent unit cells. Therefore, one corner atom contributes 1/8th of its parts to one unit cell. Since, there are eight corner atoms in a unit cell, the total number of atom contributed by the corner atom is 1/8 x 8=1. In addition, a body centered cubic unit cell has a body centered atom at the centre of the unit cell. Therefore, the total number of atoms present in a body centered cubic unit cell is two.

For FCC

In a FCC unit cell, there are eight corner atoms and six face centered atoms. A corner atom is shared by eight adjacent unit cells. Therefore, one corner atom contributes 1/8th of its parts to one unit cell. Since, there are eight corner atoms in a unit cell, the total number of atoms contributed by the corner atoms is 1/8 x 8=1. There are six face centered atoms. A face centered atom is shared by two unit cells. Therefore, one face centered atom contributes half of its parts to one unit cell. So, the total number of atoms contributed by the face centered atoms is ½ x 6 =3 and the total number of atoms present in a face centered cubic unit cell is four.
